# 📊 FashionWave: Retail Sales Performance & Strategic Insights Analysis

> **Executive Business Intelligence & Performance Report evaluating ₹2.13 Cr in revenue across 50 retail stores in India.**

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
This end-to-end retail performance analysis evaluates store-level performance, seasonal trends, product category profitability, and customer segmentation for **FashionWave**, an apparel and footwear retail brand operating across 50 stores in India.

### 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Sales Revenue:** ₹2.13 Crore
* **Gross Profit Generated:** ₹56.58 Lakhs
* **Overall Profit Margin:** 26.57%
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** ₹4,259
* **Basket Size:** 3.0 units/transaction
* **Weekday vs. Weekend Revenue Split:** 70% : 30% (Identified a 56% daily revenue gap on weekends)

---

## 🔍 Core Strategic Insights
1. **Category Profitability vs. Volume:**
   * **Accessories:** Highest margin driver at **33.05%**; recommended for checkout cross-sell bundles.
   * **Shoes:** Primary volume driver generating **₹1.33 Cr** (>60% of revenue, 5,152 units), but operates at a lower **24.10%** margin.
2. **Discount Policy & Margin Erosion:**
   * Deep discounting (>25%) pushes margins into negative territory (**-2.05%** across 683 transactions).
   * Recommended introducing a strict **15%–20% discount ceiling** to safeguard a healthy profit floor.
3. **Store Performance Variance:**
   * Top 5 stores average ₹5.2L–₹5.5L, while bottom 5 generate ₹2.9L–₹3.4L (a 45% revenue drop).
4. **Customer Segmentation:**
   * **New Explorers (48.2%):** Highest AOV (₹4,287), highly responsive to initial 15% introductory offers.
   * **Loyal Champions (24%):** Account for ₹1.01 Cr in revenue with consistent repeat purchase volume.
